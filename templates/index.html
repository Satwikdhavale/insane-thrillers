GUIDELINES & HELP

RIDER FAQ

Highly modified 4x4 vehicles with a low-range transfer case (4L) are strongly recommended for our extreme events. While AWD vehicles are welcome on soft, scenic trails, they are not permitted on the hardcore tracks. If you are unsure about your vehicle's capability, please reach out to us before registering.

Functional recovery points (front and rear) are mandatory. All-Terrain (A/T) or Mud-Terrain (M/T) tires are highly recommended. While we provide custom support and backup recovery vehicles, carrying your own basic tow strap, D-shackles, and basic tools is encouraged.

Since slots for extreme trails are highly limited, registrations are vetted. Click the "Notify Me" button on the upcoming card, fill out the form detailing your vehicle's build, and our team will get in touch with you to finalize your booking.

Your safety is our top priority. Every trail has a dedicated backup convoy consisting of at least 3 custom recovery vehicles with winches, a medical response team, and trail coordinators equipped with VHF/walkie-talkie communication devices to manage the track.
